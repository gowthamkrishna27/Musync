## ✨ Features for Listeners

### 🎧 Seamless Streaming & Discovery
* **Endless Music Catalog**: Stream millions of tracks, albums, and artists powered by YouTube Music and Audius.
* **Instant Search & Autocomplete**: Real-time smart search suggestions with instant relevance ranking.
* **Zero Audio Ads**: Enjoy uninterrupted music without commercial interruptions.
* **Gapless Background Playback**: Keeps playing with your screen off, in the car, or while using other apps.

### ⚡ Intelligent Audio & Performance
* **Dual-Band Beat Haptics**: Feel the music in your hands. The app analyzes live bass kicks and snare transients to trigger synchronized, physical haptic vibrations.
* **Proactive Next-Track Preloading**: Pre-buffers the next song in your queue for 0ms transition gaps.
* **Zero-Bandwidth Replays**: Streamed songs are automatically cached to your device (up to 200MB) so re-listening uses zero mobile data.
* **Adaptive Network Quality**: Automatically optimizes audio bitrates whether you're on 5G, Wi-Fi, LTE, or 3G to prevent buffering.

### 🎛️ Built-in Equalizer & Sound Effects
* **5-Band Equalizer**: Fine-tune frequency bands from 60Hz to 14kHz.
* **Bass Boost & Virtualizer**: Deepen sub-bass resonance and widen spatial 3D surround sound.
* **Loudness Enhancer**: Boost overall sound volume cleanly.
* **One-Tap Presets**: Choose between `Bass Boost`, `Vocal Focus`, `Treble Boost`, `Rock`, `Electronic`, `Flat`, or custom settings.

### ☁️ Cloud Sync & Privacy
* **Multiple Sign-In Options**: Sign in with **Google**, **GitHub**, or continue anonymously in **Guest Mode**.
* **Automatic Cloud Library**: Your favorites, custom playlists, and listening history stay safely backed up and synced across devices.
* **Local Music Player**: Automatically scans and plays MP3 and audio files already stored on your phone.

### 🔄 In-App Automatic Updates
* Get notified when a new update is released and install the latest features with a single tap directly inside the app.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Installation
1. [Download the latest APK](https://github.com/gowthamkrishna27/Musync/releases/latest/download/Musync.apk) on your Android device.
2. Open the downloaded `.apk` file.
3. If prompted, allow **"Install from unknown sources"** in your device settings.
4. Tap **Install** and launch **Musync**.

### 2. Permissions
* **Notifications**: Allow notification permission to control playback from your lock screen and notification shade.
* **Storage / Audio**: Allow permission if you want Musync to scan and play your local downloaded audio files.

### 3. Quick Navigation
* 🏠 **Home**: Explore trending music, quick-play playlists, and recently played songs.
* 🔍 **Search**: Search for any song, artist, album, or playlist.
* 📚 **Library**: Access your Favorites, Custom Playlists, and Local Device Audio.
* ⚙️ **Settings**: Customize audio streaming quality, toggle beat haptics, tweak equalizer presets, manage cloud sync, and check for updates.

---

## 🎚️ How to Customize Your Audio

1. Open **Settings** (Gear icon on the bottom floating bar).
2. **Beat Haptics**: Select your preferred vibration intensity (`Subtle`, `Balanced`, `Heavy`, or `Off`).
3. **Sound Equalizer**: Select a sound preset (e.g. `Bass Boost`) or manually adjust the frequency sliders.
4. **Streaming Bitrate**: Choose between `Saver`, `Low`, `Standard`, or `High` depending on your data plan.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<details>
<summary><b>Is Musync free to use?</b></summary>
Yes! Musync is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need an account to use Musync?</b></summary>
No. You can use Musync immediately in Guest Mode without logging in. Signing in with Google or GitHub is optional and allows you to sync your favorites and playlists across multiple devices.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I listen to music offline?</b></summary>
Musync automatically caches songs you listen to on your device. Tracks you've recently played can replay without using mobile data. You can also play local MP3 files stored on your phone.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I update the app?</b></summary>
Go to <b>Settings > In-App Updates</b> and tap <b>Check for Updates</b>. If a new version is available, Musync will download and prompt you to install it automatically.
</details>
